Pages and options

Every page or feature we have designed for the existing documentation site, with each option, why it exists and where it has got to.

How to read this page

  • Version: a frozen snapshot of the prototype that can be tested or reviewed. It does not change once shared.
  • Option: one idea for a single page or feature, inside a version. A version can hold several options.
  • Purpose: ideation (exploring ideas), research (ready to test with users) or handover (agreed, being passed to developers).

Recording a new page or option

  1. Add the option as a sub-folder inside the current version, or a new version if the whole site changes. Each page extends layouts/existing-docs.html and includes its content from includes/, so only what differs needs its own copy.
  2. Add a row to the table above, with its purpose and research status.
  3. Record what changed and which research finding or decision drove it on the version’s rationale page.
  4. When an option is agreed, change its purpose to handover and link the Jira ticket and developer files.
